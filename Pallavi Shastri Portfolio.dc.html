Blossom, Product design case study

A calm companion for the nine longest months

Designing a calm and supportive pregnancy companion for expectant mothers.

Role
Product Designer
Project type
Conceptual Product Design
Duration
4 weeks
Tools
Figma, FigJam
01

Overview

Pregnancy is a transformative experience filled with excitement, anticipation, and uncertainty. While there are numerous pregnancy apps available today, many of them overwhelm users with excessive information, complex navigation, and fragmented experiences.

Blossom was designed as a digital companion that supports women throughout their pregnancy journey by bringing together health tracking, educational content, appointment management, and community support in a single, easy-to-use platform.

02

The Problem

Expectant mothers often rely on multiple sources to manage their pregnancy.

Health and medication tracking apps
Medical appointment reminders
Pregnancy articles and educational resources
Online forums and communities

Switching between different platforms can create unnecessary cognitive load during a physically and emotionally demanding period.

Many existing pregnancy applications also present large amounts of information at once, making it difficult for users to focus on what matters most at their current stage of pregnancy.

How might we create a pregnancy companion that helps expectant mothers stay informed, organized, and emotionally supported without overwhelming them?

03

Understanding Users

Through secondary research and analysis of existing pregnancy products, several recurring needs emerged.

Stay informed

Users want reliable information relevant to their current stage of pregnancy rather than generic content.

Stay organized

Managing medications, supplements, doctor visits, and pregnancy milestones can become difficult without a centralized system.

Feel reassured

Many mothers experience anxiety throughout pregnancy and seek guidance that helps them feel confident and prepared.

Feel connected

Pregnancy can sometimes feel isolating. Users value spaces where they can interact with others going through similar experiences.

04

Product Goals

The solution needed to:

Simplify pregnancy management.
Reduce information overload.
Help users track important health-related activities.
Deliver personalized pregnancy insights.
Foster a sense of emotional support and community.
Create a calm and approachable user experience.
05

Competitive Analysis

While reviewing existing pregnancy applications, I observed that many products focused heavily on information delivery but often lacked simplicity.

Key opportunities
Present only the most relevant information.
Create a cleaner and less cluttered interface.
Centralize essential pregnancy tools.
Make progress and baby development easy to understand at a glance.

These opportunities became the foundation for Blossom.

06

Defining the Experience

Instead of designing another feature-heavy pregnancy app, I focused on creating a supportive companion experience. Three design principles guided the product.

Simplicity First

Users should never feel overwhelmed by information or navigation.

Personalized Guidance

Content and progress tracking should adapt to the user’s pregnancy stage.

Emotional Support

The product should feel reassuring and supportive, not clinical or intimidating.

07

Visual Design Approach

The visual language was intentionally designed to feel warm, approachable, and reassuring. Every interface decision aimed to make users feel supported rather than overwhelmed.

10

Key Learnings

01
Designing for emotional states is as important as designing for tasks

Pregnancy is not only a health journey but also an emotional one. Users need reassurance alongside functionality.

02
Less can be more

Removing unnecessary complexity helped create a more focused and approachable experience.

03
Contextual information improves engagement

Providing information that matches a user’s pregnancy stage makes content feel more useful and relevant.

11

Final Outcome

Blossom is a pregnancy companion designed to help expectant mothers navigate pregnancy with greater confidence, clarity, and support.

By combining health tracking, educational resources, appointment management, baby development insights, and community engagement into a single intuitive experience, Blossom transforms pregnancy management into a calmer and more meaningful journey.

The project demonstrates my approach to solving user problems through research-driven thinking, clear information architecture, and thoughtful user-centered design.

Blossom, because the person carrying a whole life deserves to feel supported too.

Kloud Connect

Simplifying Practice Management Through a Connected Digital Experience

01 Project overview

Kloud Connect is a cloud based practice management platform built for Australian accounting firms.

It brings together client management, workflow management, billing, timesheets, capacity planning, document management, and reporting.

The website serves as the primary digital touchpoint for prospective customers, helping them understand the platform’s capabilities, explore solutions, evaluate its relevance to their firm, and book a product demonstration.

Beyond product marketing, the website also provides educational resources through blogs, webinars, and customer success stories.

The design opportunity was to create a clear, intuitive digital experience that connects product discovery, industry education, and lead generation.

02 The challenge

Accounting firms often manage complex operations involving multiple clients, teams, workflows, billing structures, and software tools.

Evaluating a new practice management platform requires a clear understanding of its capabilities and how it fits into existing business processes.

A marketing website must communicate this complexity without overwhelming visitors with information.

From a UX perspective, the key areas to investigate were:

  • Information complexity: How can multiple product modules be communicated without overwhelming prospective customers?
  • Product discoverability: How easily can visitors find the features most relevant to their needs?
  • Trust and credibility: How can customer stories and educational resources support purchasing decisions?
  • Conversion: How can the website guide visitors from initial discovery to booking a relevant product demonstration?

These were the central design questions guiding the website evaluation.

03 Project objectives

The primary objective was to make the website more effective at communicating product value and guiding potential customers towards meaningful engagement.

Business objectives

  • Increase qualified product demonstration enquiries.
  • Communicate the platform’s value proposition clearly.
  • Build credibility with accounting firms.
  • Support product discovery through educational content.

User objectives

  • Understand what the platform does and who it serves.
  • Find relevant features without navigating excessive information.
  • Assess whether the product addresses their operational challenges.
  • Access useful industry insights and customer success stories.
  • Book a demonstration with confidence.
04 Understanding the target audience

The website serves multiple stakeholders involved in evaluating practice management software.

Primary audience

Accounting firm partners and business owners

Need visibility into firm performance, profitability, billing, and operational efficiency.

Practice managers and operations leaders

Need to understand workflows, staff capacity, deadlines, and resource allocation.

Finance and administration teams

Need clearer processes for timesheets, work in progress (WIP), invoicing, and document management.

Secondary audience

Accounting professionals researching industry solutions

Need educational content, product comparisons, practical guidance, and evidence of how modern practice management systems can improve their work.

05 Research and discovery

The discovery phase focused on understanding the website’s purpose, content structure, audience needs, and conversion journey through desk research and website review.

The review covered the homepage, product and solutions pages, company information, customer stories, blogs, and webinars.

Three key design considerations emerged from this review:

01

The product has multiple interconnected modules.

Client management, workflow, billing, timesheets, capacity planning, document management, and reporting need to be presented as parts of one connected platform rather than isolated features.

02

Visitors need more than product descriptions.

Customer success stories and educational resources help prospective customers understand the context behind the product’s value.

03

Product discovery should lead naturally to conversion.

Visitors need clear pathways from understanding the product to exploring relevant capabilities and booking a demonstration.

06 Information architecture

The proposed information architecture organises the website around the questions prospective customers are most likely to ask.

07 User journey

The intended journey moves visitors from awareness to consideration and finally to conversion.

Stage 1

Discover

The visitor lands on the homepage and learns what Kloud Connect offers and which accounting firms it serves.

Stage 2

Explore

The visitor browses product modules and solutions to understand the platform’s relevance to their operational needs.

Stage 3

Validate

The visitor reviews customer stories, webinars, and articles to build confidence and learn more about potential benefits.

Stage 4

Evaluate

The visitor identifies the relevant capabilities and considers how the platform could fit into their firm’s existing processes.

Stage 5

Convert

The visitor books a personalised product demonstration or discovery call.

A key UX consideration is maintaining a clear next step at every stage without making the experience feel overly sales-driven.

08 Content strategy

The content strategy should help visitors understand the platform at different levels of detail.

Product-led content

Explain each module through a consistent structure:

  1. 1The operational problem it addresses.
  2. 2The relevant product capability.
  3. 3The practical benefit to the accounting firm.
  4. 4A clear next step to explore or request a demo.

Knowledge hub

Use the resources section to support ongoing learning and product consideration.

Articles

Explain industry challenges, technology developments, and operational best practices.

Webinars

Offer expert-led discussions, demonstrations, and practical guidance.

Customer stories

Show how accounting firms approach implementation and improve their operational visibility.

The knowledge hub should connect relevant educational content to product pages naturally, while remaining useful to visitors who are still researching their options.

09 UX design principles

The following principles would guide any proposed redesign or optimisation.

Clarity over complexity

Use plain language, concise headings, clear page hierarchy, and scannable content to explain a complex B2B product.

Progressive disclosure

Present the most important information first, with links to detailed feature pages for visitors who want to explore further.

Consistent navigation

Use predictable labels and a clear separation between product features, solutions, resources, and company information.

Evidence-led trust

Place customer stories, relevant testimonials, integrations, and company expertise where they help visitors evaluate the product.

Conversion with context

Use clear calls to action that explain what happens next, such as booking a personalised walkthrough.

Responsive accessibility

Ensure navigation, typography, colour contrast, interactive controls, and forms work effectively across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

10 Conclusion

Kloud Connect’s website has to communicate a complex B2B software offering while serving visitors at different stages of their decision-making journey.

The central UX opportunity is to bring product clarity, educational resources, customer evidence, and conversion pathways together in a coherent experience.

By organising information around user needs, making the relationships between product modules easier to understand, and connecting useful content to relevant next steps, the website can better support both prospective customers and the business goals of Kloud Connect.

Key takeaway

Effective B2B SaaS website design is not just about presenting features. It is about helping the right audience understand value, build confidence, and make informed decisions.

07 //Screens

Creating clarity through hierarchy

Content hierarchy plays an important role in making complex financial information approachable. Structured page layouts, readable typography, and clear content groupings help visitors scan information and understand the purpose of each section.

Designing for discovery

The website brings together service information and educational resources, giving visitors multiple entry points depending on their needs. A visitor can begin with a specific service, explore a related topic, or use a practical resource to investigate a financial question.

Building trust through visual consistency

A consistent visual language across service pages and resource content helps create a unified brand experience. Photography and company information add a human dimension to a professional services website.

Supporting the journey from insight to enquiry

The experience connects educational exploration with relevant service information, helping visitors move from understanding a problem towards considering professional assistance.

08 //The knowledge hub experience

The knowledge hub complements the core corporate website by giving visitors access to educational content and practical resources.

Financial insights and articles

Articles and updates help visitors explore relevant financial and business topics. Clear content presentation makes information easier to scan and encourages further exploration.

Practical financial resources

Calculators and other tools can help visitors explore financial scenarios, estimate figures, and approach financial decisions with greater clarity.

Business growth resources

Business advisory insights provide additional context for owners navigating operational, financial, and strategic challenges.

Connecting knowledge to services

Educational content creates a natural pathway towards relevant professional support. Visitors can first explore a topic, identify a need, and then discover the advisory services that may help them.

Together, these elements position the knowledge hub as a supporting layer of the website, complementing its primary service-discovery and enquiry-generation goals.

09 //Collaboration & execution

The project involved a four-member team working across client communication, design, photography, and website implementation.

Bringing the corporate website and knowledge-oriented content together required attention to both visual consistency and information structure.

My involvement across design, client management, and implementation helped connect business requirements with the overall digital experience.

10 //Outcome

The website brings 4Front’s professional services and educational resources together within one digital presence.

Its corporate pages support service discovery and enquiries, while its articles, insights, and tools provide additional value for visitors seeking financial and business information.

This dual-purpose approach allows the website to communicate the firm’s services while supporting an ongoing relationship with users who return to explore relevant resources.

Quantitative outcomes, such as resource engagement, enquiry conversion, and returning-user rates, were not available for this case study.

11 //Key learnings

Designing for multiple user intents

The project highlighted the importance of supporting both users who are ready to seek professional help and those who need information before taking action.

Making complex information accessible

Clear content organisation and visual hierarchy are important when presenting financial topics to audiences with different levels of familiarity.

Connecting content with business goals

Educational resources can complement service discovery by helping visitors understand their needs before exploring professional support.

Thinking beyond individual pages

A corporate website and its knowledge hub work best as connected experiences rather than isolated sections, with each supporting a different stage of the user journey.

//Final reflection

The 4Front project demonstrates my experience contributing to a professional services website that combines corporate communication, financial education, and service discovery.

It strengthened my understanding of how UI/UX design, content organisation, client collaboration, and implementation work together to create a cohesive digital experience.
